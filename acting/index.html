01 About

Stage-trained, camera-ready.

Taryn Wichenko is a Toronto-based actor and singer who grew up performing in musicals in Winnipeg and never really stopped. She holds a Bachelor of Music (Honours) in Voice Performance from Western University and works across musical theatre, screen, and commercial performance.

With a background in classical voice and a deep love of storytelling, Taryn brings a strong musical instinct, emotional curiosity, and a grounded approach to every role.
